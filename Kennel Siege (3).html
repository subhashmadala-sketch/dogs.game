Kennel Siege

Every dog in the district got out of the kennel and they are all after you. Shoot them, collect what they drop and spend it between waves.

Choose a map

Money

  • Coins drop from every dog and buy the everyday weapons.
  • Azure Dots drop from the big brutes. They buy rare weapons like the Rail Gun, the Blizzard Cannon and the Tempest.
  • Apotheses drop from the Alpha that leads every fifth wave. They buy the strongest weapons, such as the Solar Beam, the Phoenix Lance and Starfall.

Special rounds

  • God Dog A giant golden dog with a huge health bar. It charges and howls for backup.
  • Juggernaut You get heavy armour and a powerful minigun until the round ends.
  • Cremate A teammate named Ash joins you with a flamethrower and sets dogs alight.
  • Purgatory Faster, stronger, tougher dogs. They also drop double.

Controls

  • Move W A S D or the arrow keys. On a touch screen, drag with your left thumb.
  • Aim and shoot Mouse, hold to fire. On a touch screen, drag with your right thumb.
  • Change weapon Keys 1 to 0, Q and E, the mouse wheel, or tap the bar under the map.
  • Pause P or Escape.
